# Guia de nomes para escritores

Use this guide to generate options without presenting invention as research.
The final choice belongs to the writer, and a plausible name is not evidence
that a real person, place, or usage existed.

## Build the naming brief

Record:

- kind: person, family, settlement, street, institution, vessel, or business;
- place and period, including whether the name is public, private, formal, or
  a nickname;
- social class, religion, language contact, migration, and pronunciation;
- narrative job: contrast, belonging, concealment, lineage, humor, or sound.

If one of these changes the shortlist, ask before presenting a confident
answer. Keep a fictional name visibly separate from a historically attested
one.

## Generate distinct families of options

For each request, vary the mechanism rather than producing eight near-spelling
variants:

1. attested given name for the period and place;
2. regional diminutive or nickname, with register noted;
3. family name connected to a documented linguistic tradition;
4. occupation, landscape, or patronymic source;
5. religious or commemorative source, when appropriate;
6. migration or language-contact form;
7. invented compound built from a stated sound or image;
8. restrained modern or unusual option only when the period allows it.

For every option, state origin or meaning, pronunciation or spelling issue,
why it fits, and what needs checking. Do not flatten a culture into a list of
"exotic" sounds.

## Check before recommending

| Check | Evidence or question |
|---|---|
| Period | Was the form in use in the requested place and time? |
| Region | Does the distribution fit the setting rather than only the language? |
| Class/register | Would this speaker use it formally, privately, or as a nickname? |
| Collisions | Does a real famous person, town, brand, or institution already use it? |
| Pronunciation | Can the intended audience read and say it as expected? |
| Sensitivity | Could the form stereotype, appropriate, or misname a group? |

Verify historical prevalence with sources; do not infer it from a name's modern
popularity. For an invented place, search the exact spelling and close variants
and report any real collision as `⚠️ verificar` until confirmed.

## Note format

Keep the shortlist compact:

| Opção | Origem/significado | Adequação ao contexto | O que verificar |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

End with two or three strongest fits, the trade-off for each, and sources. Do
not write a scene to demonstrate the names.
