# Tipografia, hierarquia e ritmo

Use this for fonts, sizes, leading, the hierarchy of titles and the rhythm of
the pages.

## Choosing the text font

- Book serifs read best in long text: Garamond, Caslon, Baskerville, Minion
  or Sabon style faces for literary fiction; sturdier faces with larger x-height
  for smaller bodies or cheaper paper.
- The font must have every PT-BR character: ã õ ç á é í ó ú â ê ô à, the
  travessão (—), en dash (–), curly quotes (“ ” ‘ ’), guillemets (« ») and
  ellipsis. Check italics and bold too.
- Licences: a commercial desktop licence may not cover ebook embedding.
  Always offer a free SIL Open Font License option. Examples of OFL text faces
  with full Latin support: EB Garamond, Crimson Pro, Libre Baskerville, Source
  Serif 4, Literata (designed for screens and ebooks), Alegreya, Spectral.
  Mark commercial fonts `⚠️ verificar licença` (print and ebook).
- Display face (titles, chapter numbers): the same family in small caps or a
  larger size is safest; a second family should contrast clearly.

## Sizes and spacing

| Element | Typical value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Body | 10.5–12 pt | 11 pt for 14 x 21; smaller for pocket editions |
| Leading | 120–140% of body | 11/15 pt is a common book setting |
| Line length | 55–70 characters | outside 45–75 characters, change format, body or margins |
| Paragraph indent | 1 em | none after titles and scene breaks |
| Notes | 8.5–9.5 pt | on their own leading, aligned to the grid where possible |
| Epigraphs, quotes | body −1 pt, indented | or italic, not both |

## Hierarchy

Define, in order: parte, capítulo (number and title), subtítulo, quebra de
cena, running head (cabeço), folio. For each give font, size, case (small
caps, capitals), alignment and space before/after in lines of the grid.

- **Chapter opener:** new page, sink about 1/3 of the page, chapter number
  and title, optional small caps for the first words or a drop cap (capitular).
- **Running heads:** author on the verso, book title (or chapter) on the
  recto; none on openers and blank pages.
- **Folios:** at the foot centred or at the outer edge; none on blank pages.
- **Scene break:** ornament, asterism (⁂) or three asterisks centred.

## Rhythm

Rhythm is how pages feel when you leaf through them: consistent openers,
predictable white space, no crowded or half-empty spreads. Check:

- facing pages end on the same line (baseline grid, no stray lines);
- no widows or orphans; no hyphen on more than 2–3 consecutive lines; no
  hyphenated word at the end of a page;
- no very short last line of a paragraph at the top of a page;
- chapter openers look the same throughout the book.

## Pre-text and post-text pages (Brazilian practice)

Pre-textuais, in order: falsa folha de rosto, (verso em branco or list of
books by the author), folha de rosto, página de créditos (copyright, ISBN,
ficha catalográfica prepared by a librarian with CRB registration, edition),
dedicatória, epígrafe, sumário when useful. Pós-textuais: agradecimentos,
sobre o autor, colofão (fonts, paper, print shop, date). Mark the ISBN and the
ficha catalográfica as items the writer must obtain; never invent them.
